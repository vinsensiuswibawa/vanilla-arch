# Tailored Resume Generator

Helps job seekers customize resumes for specific positions by analyzing job descriptions and reorganizing candidate experience to emphasize relevant qualifications.

## Key Capabilities

- **Job Description Analysis**: Extracts requirements, skills, and keywords from postings
- **Content Tailoring**: Reorganizes experience to match employer priorities
- **ATS Optimization**: Incorporates searchable keywords naturally throughout
- **Professional Formatting**: Creates clean layouts suitable for various platforms
- **Strategic Recommendations**: Identifies strengths, gaps, and interview preparation needs

## When to Use This Skill

- Applying to specific positions
- Switching industries or roles
- Optimizing for applicant tracking systems (ATS)
- Creating multiple resume versions for different job types
- Customizing for different seniority levels or companies

## How to Use

Provide either:
1. Your background information + the target job description
2. Your existing resume + the target job description

## Process

1. Gather job description and candidate background/existing resume
2. Prioritize job requirements: must-have, important, bonus
3. Map candidate experience against requirements
4. Structure resume with tailored summary, skills, and experience sections
5. Apply ATS-friendly formatting and keyword integration
6. Present formatted resume with options
7. Provide competitive analysis and gap identification
8. Offer iteration and refinement
9. Maintain truthfulness — fabricating experience is not permitted
10. Address special situations (career changes, recent graduates, executives)

## Output Format

### Professional Summary
Leads with the most relevant experience and required skills for this specific role.

### Experience Section
Reorganized to front-load achievements that match the job's priority requirements, with quantified outcomes where possible.

### Skills Section
Structured around the job's stated requirements, using the employer's exact terminology for ATS compatibility.

### Gap Analysis
Identifies unmet requirements and recommends how to address them in the cover letter or interview preparation.

## Important Guidelines

- Never fabricate experience or skills
- Use industry-standard terminology
- Use specific metrics for achievements when available
- Customize per application — no generic templates
- Do not include personal details unrelated to the role
- Do not overstate qualifications beyond actual experience
